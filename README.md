# ABX Loan Calculator
This is a simple calculator for loans on [AlphBanX](https://www.alphbanx.com/).

To use:
- Enter Collateral in ALPH
- Enter Borrow ABD
- Select Interest Rate

On the left side of the window, the calculator will show the CR in % and indicate its danger/safety, as well as the price at which your loan will be liquidated.

On the right side, the calculator will show the interest on the loan in ALPH and USD for different periods of time.
